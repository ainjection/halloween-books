For little monsters, ages 4 to 8

Can you find all 12?

Fifty busy Halloween scenes, each hiding twelve little things to spot, from a trick-or-treat street to a ghost ship and a skeleton dance party. Find them, tick them off, then colour the whole page in. Friendly, never frightening.

Mystery Mosaics, ages 6 to 12

Colour the dots. Who's hiding?

Every page is a wall of numbered circles. Colour each one to match its number, and a picture slowly appears: a vampire boy, a fox in a pumpkin, a friendly ghost. Try this little one. Click the circles and see what turns up.

Big-print word searches, for grown-ups and seniors

Big letters. No tricks.

Eighty Halloween word searches in large, easy-to-read print. The Easy book keeps words running forwards; the Hard one hides them in all eight directions. Have a go at this one: click the first letter of a word, then the last.

For grown-up ghouls

Grayscale, by candlelight.

Detailed shaded designs made for coloured pencils: owls on gates, haunted manors, a skeleton violinist, witches over rooftops. The grey shading does the hard work, so a little colour goes a long way. Move your mouse over the page to see what we mean.
